# Vocal Separation Guides

Practical listening exercises for people working with isolated vocals and backing tracks.

## Tutorials

[How to judge a vocal separation by ear](guides/judge-vocal-separation.md) — Compare three short passages across the original recording, the instrumental, and the vocal stem. Includes a worked listening log and decision criteria for rehearsal, remixing, and transcription.

[How to practice singing with isolated vocals and a backing track](guides/practice-singing-with-isolated-vocals.md) — Study one phrase, rehearse without the guide vocal, record a take, and choose one concrete improvement.

[How to practice drums with a drumless backing track](guides/practice-drums-with-drumless-tracks.md) — Audition a drumless mix, practice a groove and its transition, and review timing and dynamics with a reusable listening log.

## About these guides

Maintained by VocalRemover.one. The exercises work with suitable stems from any separation tool. Product links point to our own tools; illustrative practice logs are examples rather than audio test results.
